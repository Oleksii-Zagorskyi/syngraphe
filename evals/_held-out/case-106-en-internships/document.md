# Internship Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

An internship is a fixed-term learning placement of a person who is enrolled as a student or who graduated no more than 12 months before the start date.
This policy applies to every internship in every department of the Organisation.

## 2. Who may host an intern

A department may host an intern only when the approving officer has approved its hosting request.
The approving officer is the People Team Lead, except for an internship hosted by the People Team, where the approving officer is the Operations Director instead.
The head of the department submits the hosting request to the approving officer at least 20 working days before the proposed start date.
The approving officer approves the request when the department meets both of the following conditions and refuses it otherwise.

- The department has an employee who meets the mentor requirements in Section 5.
- The department would have no more than two interns at one time, counting the intern requested.

The approving officer gives the decision to the head of the department in writing within 10 working days of receiving the request.
For decisions under this policy, the approving officer answers to the Executive Director.

## 3. Duration

An internship lasts a whole number of weeks, no fewer than 4 and no more than 26.
The agreed duration is stated in the internship agreement, which the intern and the approving officer sign before the start date.
The approving officer may extend an internship once, in writing, at the request of the head of the host department and under the conditions of Section 2, to a new agreed duration within the limits above.
The intern may end the internship early by giving the approving officer 5 working days' written notice.
The head of the host department may end the internship early only with the written approval of the approving officer, who then gives the intern 5 working days' written notice.

## 4. Stipend

The Organisation pays every intern a weekly stipend at a rate that depends on the agreed duration stated in the internship agreement.

- For 4 to 8 weeks inclusive, the rate is 300 crowns per week.
- For 10 to 16 weeks inclusive, the rate is 350 crowns per week.
- For 17 to 26 weeks inclusive, the rate is 400 crowns per week.

When an internship is extended under Section 3, the rate for the new agreed duration applies from the first day of the extension, and the stipend for earlier weeks is not recalculated.
When an internship ends early under Section 3, the rate does not change, and the intern receives one fifth of the weekly rate for each working day of the unfinished week up to and including the last day.
The Payroll Officer pays the stipend on the last working day of each calendar month for the part of the internship that fell in that month.

## 5. Mentor

The head of the host department appoints one mentor for each intern at least 10 working days before the start date.
A mentor meets all of the following requirements.

- The mentor is an employee of the host department and is not its head.
- The mentor has worked at the Organisation for at least two years.
- The mentor is not the mentor of another intern at the same time.

The mentor writes a learning plan, which lists the learning goals and the department tools the intern will use, and submits it to the head of the host department at least 5 working days before the start date.
The head of the host department, as approving officer, approves the learning plan or returns it once with comments to the mentor, who corrects it, and approves the corrected plan before the start date.
The mentor meets the intern once a week to review progress against the learning plan.

## 6. End-of-internship assessment

The mentor writes an assessment of the intern against the learning plan and submits it to the head of the host department no later than the last day of the internship.
When the internship ends early under Section 3, the mentor submits the assessment within 5 working days after the last day instead.
The head of the host department confirms the assessment, or returns it once with comments, within 5 working days of receiving it.
The mentor revises a returned assessment within 3 working days, and the head of the host department then confirms it.
The head of the host department gives the intern a copy of the confirmed assessment within 5 working days of confirming it.

## 7. Access to systems

The mentor requests the intern's access from the IT Service Manager at least 3 working days before the start date.
The IT Service Manager grants the following access on the start date.

- The intern receives an email account of the Organisation.
- The intern receives access to the shared document workspace of the host department.
- The intern receives access to the department tools listed in the approved learning plan.

The IT Service Manager does not grant an intern administrator rights or access to payroll data, personnel records or customer payment data.
The approving officer informs the IT Service Manager and the Payroll Officer of an extension or an early ending on the day it is approved or notified.
The IT Service Manager removes all of the intern's access by the end of the last day of the internship.
