# Company Phone Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for issuing, using and returning company phones and applies to every employee of the Organisation.
The Managing Director approves this policy.
The Head of IT reviews it once a year and answers to the Managing Director for its application.

## Definitions

A company phone is a mobile phone owned by the Organisation, together with its SIM card and its charger.
A holder is an employee to whom a company phone has been issued.
The monthly charges are the amount that the mobile operator invoices for one company phone for one calendar month.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday.

## Who gets a phone

An employee is eligible for a company phone if their employment contract includes on-call duty or work outside the Organisation's premises.
An employee whose employment contract includes neither of these duties does not receive a company phone.
The HR Manager checks eligibility against the employment contract and sends a written confirmation to the IT service desk.
The HR Manager does not confirm their own eligibility; the Managing Director confirms it instead.
The IT service desk issues one company phone to the employee within three working days of receiving the confirmation.

## Monthly plan limit

The monthly plan limit is 25 euros for each company phone.
The Organisation pays monthly charges up to and including 25 euros.
When the monthly charges are above 25 euros, the amount above 25 euros is the excess.
The IT service desk sends a statement of the excess to the holder and to the Finance Director within five working days of receiving the operator's invoice.
The holder may send the Finance Director a written explanation of the excess within five working days of receiving the statement.
The Finance Director decides within five working days of receiving the explanation what amount of the monthly charges arose from personal use.
The holder pays that amount, up to the amount of the excess, and the Organisation pays the remaining part of the excess.
A holder who sends no explanation within the five working days pays the whole excess.
The Finance Director deducts the amount that the holder pays from the holder's next salary payment.
When the holder is the Finance Director, the Managing Director receives the statement and the explanation and makes the decision instead.

## Personal use

A holder may use the company phone for personal calls, messages and mobile data.
Charges from personal use count towards the monthly charges, and the section "Monthly plan limit" applies to the excess.
A holder must not call premium-rate numbers, buy paid subscriptions through the operator, or lend the company phone to another person.
Each operator invoice is checked for premium-rate calls and paid subscriptions.

## Loss or damage

In this policy, loss means that the holder no longer has the company phone, whether it was mislaid or stolen.
The holder reports loss to the IT service desk within four hours of noticing it.
The IT service desk blocks the SIM card and erases the lost phone remotely within one hour of receiving the report.
The IT service desk issues a replacement within three working days of the report.
A holder whose phone is lost through their own negligence, as the Finance Director decides, pays half the price of the replacement.
The holder hands a damaged phone to the IT service desk within two working days of noticing the damage.
If the repair estimate is below half of the price of a new phone of the same model, the IT service desk has the phone repaired.
If the repair estimate is half of that price or more, the IT service desk issues a replacement instead.
The Organisation bears the cost of every repair and every replacement.

## Return on leaving

A holder who leaves the Organisation returns the company phone to the IT service desk no later than their last working day, which for an on-call holder may be a Saturday or a Sunday.
A holder who stops being eligible returns it within five working days of the date on which the contract change takes effect.
The IT service desk erases the returned phone and signs the return record.
If the company phone is not returned by the deadline set in this section, the IT service desk blocks it on the next working day and informs the Finance Director.
The Finance Director then deducts the residual value of the phone, as recorded in the asset register, from the holder's next payment from the Organisation.

## The IT service desk

The IT service desk issues, replaces, blocks and takes back company phones, and keeps the asset register and the return records.
The IT service desk works from 08:00 to 18:00 on working days at extension 400 and accepts reports of loss at any hour at extension 499.
The Head of IT manages the IT service desk and answers to the Managing Director for its work under this policy.
