# Safety Instruction for Work on Roof Gutters

## 1. Scope

This instruction applies to every employee of the Organisation who takes part in gutter work on its buildings.

Gutter work means cleaning, inspecting and repairing roof gutters and downpipes from outside the building.

All gutter work is treated as work at height, whatever the height of the eaves.

## 2. Roles

The operative is the employee who goes up and does the gutter work.

The attendant is the second person, who stays on the ground beside the access equipment while the operative is above the ground.

The site supervisor answers to the safety officer for every permit issued.

The safety officer reports to the director of the Organisation.

The site supervisor does not act as operative or attendant.

## 3. Permit

Gutter work does not start without a written permit issued by the site supervisor.

The site supervisor issues one permit for one building and one work shift.

The site supervisor writes in the permit the building, the date, the height of the eaves, the access equipment, the operative and the attendant.

The operative and the attendant sign the permit before the work starts.

The site supervisor hands every closed permit to the safety officer within two working days.

The safety officer reviews the closed permits once a month and reports each breach of this instruction to the director.

## 4. Equipment

Gutter work on eaves below 2 metres is not work at height and needs no permit.
For eaves up to and including 4 metres above the ground, the operative uses an extension ladder with a stabiliser.

For eaves more than 4 metres above the ground, the operative uses the mobile work platform instead of a ladder.

The site supervisor issues to the operative a helmet, non-slip footwear and gloves.

The site supervisor issues a safety harness with a lanyard to an operative who uses the mobile work platform.

The site supervisor issues to the attendant a helmet, a wind meter and a charged telephone.

The operative inspects the access equipment, and the operative and the attendant each inspect the items issued to them, before each use.

The person who finds damage to the access equipment or to an issued item reports it to the site supervisor, and the operative does not go up until the site supervisor has provided a replacement.

## 5. Weather limits

The site supervisor checks the weather at the building and does not issue the permit when any of the following conditions is present.

- The wind speed at ground level beside the building is 10 metres per second or more.
- Rain, snow or sleet is falling.
- There is ice or frost on the roof or on the access equipment.
- Thunder is heard or lightning is seen.
- The air temperature is below minus 5 degrees Celsius.

The attendant measures the wind speed every 30 minutes during the work and watches for the other conditions.

When any of these conditions appears during the work, the attendant stops the work under section 8.

## 6. Order of the work

The operative and the attendant carry out the work in the following order.

- The attendant fences off the ground under the work area with barrier tape.
- The operative sets up the access equipment on firm, level ground.
- The operative who uses a ladder ties its top to the building before starting the gutter work.
- The operative who uses the mobile work platform fastens the lanyard to its anchor point before the platform rises.
- The attendant holds the foot of the ladder each time the operative climbs or descends.
- The operative keeps both feet on the ladder or on the platform floor and does not step onto the roof.
- The operative comes down before the access equipment is moved to the next section of the gutter.
- The operative reports the end of the work to the site supervisor, who closes the permit.

## 7. Second person present

The operative does not go up unless the attendant is in place beside the access equipment.

When the attendant has to leave, the attendant first tells the operative to come down, and the operative stays on the ground until the attendant returns.

## 8. Stopping work and accidents

The operative and the attendant each stop the work on seeing a danger.

When the work is stopped under this section, the operative comes down at once and the attendant informs the site supervisor.

The site supervisor allows work stopped under this section to resume under the same permit only when the danger has passed, no condition listed in section 5 is present and the work shift has not ended, and records the time of resumption in the permit.

When the operative falls or is injured, the attendant calls the emergency service and then informs the site supervisor.

The site supervisor reports the accident to the safety officer on the same day.

After an accident, the access equipment is taken out of use until it has been examined.
