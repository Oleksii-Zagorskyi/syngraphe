# Boiler Room Inspection Checklist

## 1. Purpose and scope

This checklist governs the weekly inspection and the annual inspection of the boiler room in the main building of the Organisation.
The boiler room holds two hot-water boilers, named Boiler 1 and Boiler 2.
Repairs and the choice of contractors are outside this checklist.

## 2. Roles

The boiler operator carries out the weekly inspection.
The facilities engineer assigns one boiler operator to each weekly inspection and carries out the annual inspection.
The operations director supervises the facilities engineer.
In this checklist, "the inspector" means the boiler operator during a weekly inspection and the facilities engineer during an annual inspection.

## 3. Limits

The inspector takes readings only from a boiler that is running.
For a boiler that is shut down, the inspector writes "shut down" in the logbook in place of each reading.

The pressure of a running boiler is within limits from 1.5 bar to 3.0 bar, both values included.
A pressure above 3.0 bar is an out-of-limit reading.

The flow temperature of a running boiler is within limits from 60 °C to 85 °C, both values included.
A flow temperature below 60 °C or above 85 °C is an out-of-limit reading.

## 4. Weekly inspection

The boiler operator carries out the weekly inspection every Monday before 10:00.
When Monday is not a working day, the boiler operator carries it out before 10:00 on the first working day after that Monday.

The boiler operator checks the room items once, in the order listed.

- Check that the boiler room door closes fully and locks.
- Check that both ventilation grilles are free of obstruction.
- Check that the floor is free of water and oil.
- Check that the fire extinguisher is in its bracket and its gauge needle is in the green zone.

The boiler operator then checks the boiler items in the order listed, first for Boiler 1 and then for Boiler 2.

- Read the pressure gauge and record the reading.
- Read the flow temperature gauge and record the reading.
- Check that the seal on the safety valve is unbroken.
- Check that the emergency stop button is free of obstruction and its label is legible.

## 5. Annual inspection

The facilities engineer carries out the annual inspection once every year, in September.
The facilities engineer checks all the items of section 4 in the same order.
After each boiler's items from section 4, the facilities engineer checks the additional items for that boiler in the order listed.

- Check that the date on the calibration label of the pressure gauge has not passed.
- Check the burner housing for soot and corrosion.
- Check the flue joints for gaps and staining.
- Check that the date on the safety valve certificate has not passed.

The boiler operator still carries out the weekly inspection in the week of the annual inspection.

## 6. Out-of-limit reading

On an out-of-limit reading, the inspector waits 10 minutes and takes a second reading from the same gauge before continuing.
If the second reading is within limits, the inspector records both readings and continues the inspection.
If the second reading is out of limit, the inspector shuts the boiler down with the emergency stop button and records both readings.

On a first reading above 3.5 bar or above 80 °C, the inspector shuts the boiler down at once instead of waiting for a second reading.

After a shutdown during a weekly inspection, the boiler operator telephones the facilities engineer at once and then completes the remaining items.
The boiler operator restarts that boiler only after the facilities engineer has written a restart authorisation in the logbook.

After a shutdown during an annual inspection, the facilities engineer telephones the operations director at once and then completes the remaining items.
The facilities engineer restarts that boiler only after the operations director has written a restart authorisation in the logbook.

## 7. Defects

A defect is any item of section 4 or section 5, other than a reading, that is not satisfied.
The boiler operator records each defect found in a weekly inspection and reports it to the facilities engineer on the same working day.
The facilities engineer decides the remedy for that defect and writes the decision in the logbook.
The facilities engineer records each defect found in an annual inspection and reports it to the operations director on the same working day.
The operations director decides the remedy for that defect and writes the decision in the logbook.

## 8. Logbook

The facilities engineer keeps the logbook on the shelf beside the boiler room door.
For each inspection, the inspector enters the date, the start time, the result of every item, every reading and a signature.
The facilities engineer reviews and countersigns each weekly entry within two working days.
The operations director reviews and countersigns each annual entry within five working days.
Each completed logbook is stored for five years after its last entry.
