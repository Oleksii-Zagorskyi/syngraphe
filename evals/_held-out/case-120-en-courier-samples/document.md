# Work Instruction: Sending Samples by Courier

## Scope

This instruction applies to every sample that the Organisation sends from its Dispatch Room to an outside laboratory by courier.
The Sample Owner is the staff member who asks for a sample to be sent.

## Categories

The Sample Owner assigns each sample to one category, using the storage temperature written on the sample record.
- A sample stored at or below minus 15 °C is Frozen.
- A sample stored above minus 15 °C and at or below 8 °C is Chilled.
- A sample stored above 8 °C is Ambient.

The Sample Owner sends a separate dispatch request to the Dispatch Room for each category.
One box holds samples of one category only.

## Courier booking

On the day the dispatch request arrives, the Dispatch Technician books in the courier's portal the earliest collection day that the courier offers and that this section allows for the category.
- An Ambient box may be collected on any day on which the courier collects, and travels by the standard service.
- A Chilled or Frozen box may be collected only on a Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday, and travels by the next-day service.

This rule does not apply to a box that the Sample Owner marks as urgent on the dispatch request.
The Dispatch Technician writes the booking reference on the dispatch note.

## Packaging by category

The Dispatch Technician places each sample, in its sealed container, inside a leakproof bag with one absorbent sheet, and packs the bags by category.
- Ambient samples go into a rigid cardboard box with padding and no cooling packs.
- Chilled samples go into an insulated box with four cooling packs.
- Frozen samples go into an insulated box with six cooling packs.

The Dispatch Technician packs an Ambient box at any time between the booking and the collection.
The Dispatch Technician packs a Chilled or Frozen box on the collection day, not earlier than two hours before the booked collection window begins.

## Cooling packs

The Dispatch Technician conditions cooling packs before use for at least 12 hours in the refrigerator for Chilled samples and for at least 24 hours in the freezer for Frozen samples.
The Dispatch Technician takes the cooling packs out not more than 15 minutes before the box is closed.
The Dispatch Technician starts one temperature logger and places it in each Chilled or Frozen box.

## Labels

The Dispatch Technician attaches all labels.
Each container carries a label with the sample number and the category.
Each box carries on its outside the address of the receiving laboratory, the return address of the Organisation, the booking reference, the category and arrows that show which side is up.
A Chilled or Frozen box carries, in addition, a label with the words "Keep cold".

## Documents with the box

The Sample Owner attaches to the dispatch request a dispatch note with the sample numbers, the category and the receiving laboratory.
The Dispatch Technician adds the number of cooling packs and the number of the temperature logger to the dispatch note for a Chilled or Frozen box.
The Dispatch Supervisor compares the open packed box with the dispatch note and with this instruction.
If the box is wrong, the Dispatch Technician corrects it, and if the dispatch note is wrong, the Sample Owner corrects it.
The Dispatch Supervisor compares again after each correction and signs the dispatch note when no difference remains.
The Dispatch Technician closes the box only after that signature.
The Dispatch Technician puts one copy of the signed dispatch note inside the box and a second copy in a pouch on its outside.

## Hand-over signature

Before handing the box to the courier, the Dispatch Technician compares the booking reference shown by the courier with the one on the dispatch note.
The courier writes name, date and time in the hand-over log and signs it, and the Dispatch Technician signs the same line.
If the booking references differ, or if the courier does not sign, the Dispatch Technician does not hand over the box, and the time limits of the section "Uncollected boxes" continue to run for it.

## Uncollected boxes

If an Ambient box has not been handed over by the end of the collection day, the Dispatch Technician keeps it closed and books a new collection under the section "Courier booking".
If a Chilled box has not been handed over within four hours after it was closed, the Dispatch Technician opens it, returns the samples to their storage and books a new collection under the section "Courier booking".
The Dispatch Technician packs those samples again on the new collection day, and the Dispatch Supervisor compares and signs again under the section "Documents with the box".
