# Site Rules on Visitors

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to every visitor and to every employee who deals with a visitor.
The Site Manager is responsible for the application of these rules and reports on it to the Operations Director.

## 2. Terms

The site is the buildings and grounds of the Organisation inside the perimeter fence.
A visitor is any person who enters the site and is not an employee.
A contractor is a visitor who performs work on the site under a contract with the Organisation.
A guest is a visitor who is not a contractor.
A host is the employee who answers for one visitor during one entry to the site.
Working hours are 08:00 to 17:00 from Monday to Friday.
Every rule for visitors applies to guests and to contractors alike, except where section 7 or section 8 sets a different rule for contractors.

## 3. Registration at reception

Reception is staffed by a receptionist at all hours.
The host submits a visit request to the receptionist before the visitor arrives.
The receptionist refuses entry to a visitor for whom no visit request has been submitted.
The receptionist registers a guest only during working hours and refuses entry to a guest at any other time.
The receptionist registers a contractor only within the working hours named in the work permit and refuses entry to a contractor at any other time.
Every visitor registers at reception on each entry, before going to any other part of the site.
The visitor shows the receptionist an identity document with a photograph, and the receptionist refuses entry to a visitor who does not show one.
The receptionist records in the visitor register the name of the visitor, the name of the host and the time of arrival.
The receptionist then calls the host, and the visitor waits in the reception hall until the host arrives.

## 4. Host

The host of a guest is the employee who invited the guest.
The host of a contractor is the employee who ordered the work.
The host collects the visitor from the reception hall and brings the visitor back to reception at the end of the visit.
A host who has to leave the site while the visitor is still on it hands the visitor over to another employee and informs the receptionist, who records that employee in the visitor register as the new host.

## 5. Badges

The receptionist issues a yellow badge to each guest and a blue badge to each contractor at registration.
A badge is valid for one entry.
The visitor wears the badge where it can be seen for the whole time on the site.
The visitor returns the badge to the receptionist when leaving, and the receptionist records the time of departure in the visitor register.

## 6. Open area and restricted area

The open area consists of the reception hall, the ground-floor meeting rooms and the canteen.
The restricted area is every other part of the site.
A visitor may move in the open area without the host.
In the restricted area the host stays with the visitor at all times, except as section 7 allows for a contractor inside the work zone.

## 7. Contractors working on site

A contractor works on the site only under a work permit.
The Facilities Manager issues the work permit at the request of the host.
When the Facilities Manager is the host, the Site Manager issues the work permit instead.
The work permit names the contractor, the work zone, the dates of the work and the working hours on each date.
The host gives a copy of the work permit to the receptionist together with the visit request.
The host brings the contractor to the work zone and gives the contractor a safety briefing on that zone before the work begins.
Inside the work zone the contractor may work without the host.

## 8. Leaving times

A guest leaves the site by 18:00 on the day of arrival.
A guest who attends a training day on a Saturday registers at reception between 08:00 and 10:00 on that Saturday.
A guest may stay after 18:00 only if the Site Manager, at the request of the host, sends the receptionist written approval of a later leaving time before 17:00 on the same day.
When the Site Manager is the host, the Operations Director gives this approval instead.
A contractor leaves the site by the end of working hours, instead of by 18:00.
When the leaving time of a visitor has passed and the badge has not been returned, the receptionist informs the host named in the visit request at once.
The host then brings the visitor to reception without delay.
