# Annual Performance Review Process

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document describes how the Organisation reviews employees' performance and how the result sets the performance bonus.
The review year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
The process applies to every employee employed by the Organisation on 31 December of the review year and for at least 90 calendar days of that year, even if the employment ends after that date.
An employee who does not meet both conditions receives no rating and no performance bonus for that year.
The Director General and the employees who report directly to the Director General are outside this process.

## 2. Roles

The line manager of an employee is the person to whom the employee reports on 31 December of the review year.
The HR Lead answers to the Director General for the duties this document gives the HR Lead.
The Calibration Panel consists of the HR Lead and all heads of departments, and answers to the Director General for its decisions.
The Appeals Committee consists of three employees, not members of the Calibration Panel, whom the Director General appoints by 31 January, and answers to the Director General for its decisions.

## 3. The annual cycle

The Organisation reviews performance once for each review year, in the stages of sections 4 to 8.
A date given without a year in sections 4 to 8 is in the year that follows the review year.
When a date in this document falls on a non-working day, the time limit ends on the next working day.

## 4. Self-assessment

The HR Lead sends each employee in scope a review form by 5 January.
The employee describes in the form the results achieved against the duties in the job description and submits the form to the line manager by 20 January.
If the employee does not submit the form by 20 January, the line manager continues without it and records that fact in the form.

## 5. The line manager's rating

The line manager reads any submitted self-assessment and holds a review conversation with the employee.
The line manager then records one proposed rating with written reasons in the review form and sends it to the HR Lead by 5 February.
The proposed rating is one of the four ratings below.

- Exceptional means the employee reached the required results in every duty and exceeded them in more than half.
- Strong means the employee reached the required results in every duty and exceeded them in at least one duty but not in more than half.
- Satisfactory means the employee reached the required results in every duty and exceeded them in none.
- Below expectations means the employee did not reach the required results in one or more duties.

## 6. The calibration meeting

The HR Lead convenes and chairs the calibration meeting of the Calibration Panel between 6 February and 20 February.
For each employee the Calibration Panel either confirms the proposed rating or replaces it with another of the four ratings.
A member who proposed a rating as line manager takes no part in the decision on that rating.
The Calibration Panel replaces the proposed rating only with a rating that more than half of the members taking part vote for, and otherwise confirms it.
The rating that the Calibration Panel confirms or sets is the final rating.
The HR Lead records the final rating and the panel's reasons in the review form.
The line manager tells the employee the final rating and its reasons by the last day of February and hands over a copy of the review form that day.

## 7. Appeal

An employee may appeal against the final rating in writing to the HR Lead within 10 working days after receiving the copy of the review form.
The HR Lead rejects an appeal submitted after that time limit, and the final rating stands.
The HR Lead passes an appeal submitted in time to the Appeals Committee on the next working day.
The HR Lead decides an appeal of a head of department alone, instead of the Appeals Committee.
If a member of the Appeals Committee is the appellant or the appellant's line manager, the Director General appoints another employee who meets the conditions of section 2 in that member's place for that appeal.
The Appeals Committee decides within 15 working days after the HR Lead received the appeal.
The Appeals Committee confirms the final rating unless at least two of its three members vote for one other of the four ratings, which then becomes the final rating.
The HR Lead informs the employee and the line manager of the decision in writing within three working days after it is made.

## 8. Performance bonus

The performance bonus is a percentage of the base salary paid to the employee for the review year.

- An employee rated Exceptional receives 20 per cent.
- An employee rated Strong receives 12 per cent.
- An employee rated Satisfactory receives 5 per cent.
- An employee rated Below expectations receives no performance bonus.

The HR Lead sends the Payroll Accountant the list of final ratings by 15 April, after all appeals are decided.
The Payroll Accountant calculates each performance bonus from that list and pays it by 30 April.
Each review form is kept for three years after 30 April.
