# Procedure for Requesting and Removing Access to IT Systems

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how access to the IT systems of the Organisation is requested, approved, granted, recorded, reviewed and removed.
It applies to every employee of the Organisation and to every system.
A system is any application, database, device or network service that the Organisation operates or subscribes to.
Access is the ability of an employee to sign in to a system at a stated access level.
The access register is the single record of all access, kept by the Service Desk.
A role is the job position that an employee holds in the Organisation.

## 2. Participants

The requester is the employee who needs the access.
The line manager is the person to whom the requester reports.
For a request made by the Managing Director, the Chair of the Board acts as the line manager.
For a request made by the Chair of the Board, the Managing Director acts as the line manager.
The Head of Information Technology appoints one system owner and one alternate owner for every application and every database and has the Service Desk enter those appointments in the access register.
The Head of Information Technology and the Managing Director are not appointed as system owners.
The alternate owner is an employee who holds no access to that system, does not request it, and is neither the system owner nor the line manager of the system owner.
The Service Desk administrator is the employee of the Service Desk who grants and removes access.

## 3. Requesting access

The requester submits an access request to the Service Desk on the access request form.
The requester states on the form the system, the access level needed and the work reason for the access.
The requester also submits an access request to change an access level that the requester already holds.
Each access request form is kept for two years after the access is granted or the request is refused.
The Service Desk sends the request to the line manager on the working day it receives the request.

## 4. Approval

The line manager decides first whether the access is needed for the requester's work.
When the line manager approves, the Service Desk sends the request to the person who gives the second decision.
The system owner gives the second decision: whether the access level is appropriate for the system.
When the requester or the line manager is the system owner, the alternate owner gives the second decision instead.
When the line manager or the person giving the second decision refuses, the Service Desk closes the request and informs the requester of the refusal and its reason.

## 5. Granting and recording access

A Service Desk administrator grants the access within two working days after the second decision is an approval.
A Service Desk administrator does not grant access to himself or herself; another Service Desk administrator grants it.
On the day the access is granted, the Service Desk administrator who granted it records the following in the access register.

- The requester and the system.
- The access level granted.
- The line manager and the person who gave the second decision.
- The date on which the access was granted.

The same Service Desk administrator informs the requester that the access is active.

## 6. Periodic review

On the first working day of January and of July, the Service Desk sends the system owner and the alternate owner of each system the list of all access to that system from the access register.
Within fifteen working days, the system owner confirms each access on the list or instructs the Service Desk to remove it, and returns the list to the Service Desk.
The system owner does not review his or her own access; the alternate owner reviews it in the same way within the same fifteen working days.
The Service Desk reports each list not returned in time to the alternate owner, who then completes that review within ten working days of the report.
A Service Desk administrator removes each access that the review instructs to remove within two working days after the Service Desk receives the instruction and records the removal and its date in the access register.

## 7. Removal on a change of role

When an employee changes role, the person who was the line manager before the change notifies the Service Desk no later than the day the change takes effect.
A Service Desk administrator removes all access of that employee within one working day after the change takes effect and records the removal and its date in the access register.
The employee requests the access needed for the new role under section 3.

## 8. Removal on leaving

When an employee leaves the Organisation, the Human Resources officer notifies the Service Desk of the last working day no later than that day.
A Service Desk administrator removes all access of that employee by the end of the last working day and records the removal and its date in the access register.
