# Travel expenses

## Scope

These rules apply to every employee who travels for work.

## Approval

An employee asks for approval of a trip at least ten working days before it starts.
A trip that is asked for later is not approved.
The head of the employee's department approves a trip inside the country.
The director approves a trip abroad.
The director approves each trip of a head of department, instead of the head of the department.
The chair of the board approves each trip of the director, instead of the director.
The director decides on an urgent trip that is asked for later.

## Costs

The Organisation pays only for a trip that was approved.
The Organisation pays for a seat in economy class.
The Organisation pays for public transport at the place of the trip.
The Organisation pays for one hotel night for each night of the trip.
The Organisation pays a fixed amount of 40 euros for meals for each day of the trip.
Taxis are paid only when public transport is not reasonable.
The person who approved the trip decides if public transport is not reasonable.

## Claims

An employee submits a claim within 30 days after the trip ends.
A claim submitted later is not paid.
The claim includes a receipt for each cost except meals.
The finance department pays the claim within fifteen working days after it receives the claim.
