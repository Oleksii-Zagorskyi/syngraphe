# Annual Leave Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for paid annual leave at the Organisation.
It applies to every employee of the Organisation, full-time and part-time, from the first day of employment to the last.
It does not govern sick leave, parental leave or unpaid leave.

## Terms

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday.
A leave day is a working day on which the employee is contracted to work.
The leave register is the Organisation's record of entitlements, requests and decisions.
The remaining entitlement for a leave year is the employee's entitlement for that leave year, plus the amount carried over into it, minus the leave days in it already taken or approved.

## Entitlement and counting

An employee contracted to work five days a week is entitled to 25 leave days in a leave year.
An employee contracted to work fewer than five days a week is entitled to 25 leave days multiplied by the contracted days per week and divided by five.
An employee employed for only part of a leave year is entitled to a share of that amount in proportion to the calendar days employed in it.
When the contracted days per week change during a leave year, the entitlement is calculated separately for each period and the unrounded results are added.
Only the final result is rounded up, to the nearest half day.
The Head of People calculates each entitlement and records it in the leave register within five working days of the start of employment, of a contract change, or of the start of a leave year.
The Head of People also records in the leave register each amount carried over into a leave year.
One leave day is deducted for each leave day of absence, and half a leave day for an absence of half of the contracted hours of that day.
Leave is taken in whole or half leave days only.

## Requesting leave

The employee submits each request in the leave register, stating the first and the last day of leave.
A request for fewer than five leave days is submitted at least 10 working days before the first day of leave.
A request for more than five leave days is submitted at least 30 working days before the first day of leave.
A request for more than five leave days that is submitted at least 20 working days before the first day of leave is in time.

## Approval

The line manager of the employee decides each request.
The requests of an employee who manages other employees are decided by that employee's own line manager.
The Chair of the Board decides the Director's requests.
When the line manager is absent, the Head of People decides the request instead.
The approver records the decision in the leave register within five working days of the request.
The approver refuses a request that is submitted later than the time limit that applies to it, or in which the leave days of a leave year exceed the remaining entitlement for that leave year, and states the reason in the leave register.
The approver approves every other request.
A request with no recorded decision after five working days counts as approved.

## Carry-over

At the end of 31 December, the remaining entitlement for the ending leave year of an employee whose employment continues on 1 January carries over to the next leave year, up to five leave days.
Any part of it above five leave days lapses at the same moment without payment.
A remaining entitlement below zero carries over in full.
The carried-over amount is recorded in the leave register by 15 January.

## Leave during the notice period

The notice period starts on the day the employee or the Organisation gives written notice of termination and ends on the last day of employment.
The Head of People recalculates the entitlement up to the last day of employment within three working days of the start of the notice period.
Leave approved before the notice period remains approved.
The Payroll Officer pays, with the final salary, the remaining entitlement for the leave year of the last day of employment as it stands at the end of that day.
The Payroll Officer deducts from the final salary the pay for each leave day by which that remaining entitlement is below zero.
