# Policy on the Use of Company Vehicles

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for using the vehicles that the Organisation owns or leases.
It applies to every employee of the Organisation and to every such vehicle.

## 2. Fleet management

The Operations Director appoints the Fleet Manager.
The Fleet Manager manages the fleet and keeps the booking register, the list of authorised employees and the mileage log.
The Fleet Manager arranges the registration, insurance, periodic inspection, servicing and repair of every vehicle.
When the Fleet Manager is absent, the Operations Director performs the Fleet Manager's duties.
Where the Fleet Manager wishes to use a vehicle, the Operations Director performs, for that authorisation and that trip, every duty that sections 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9 give to the Fleet Manager.

## 3. Who may use a vehicle

Only an authorised employee may drive a vehicle.
The Fleet Manager authorises only an employee whose valid driving licence for the class of vehicle the Fleet Manager has checked.
An authorised employee whose licence is suspended, withdrawn or expired informs the Fleet Manager on the same day.
The Fleet Manager then removes that employee from the list of authorised employees.
In this policy, the "driver" means the authorised employee who holds an approved booking for a vehicle.
Only the driver may drive the vehicle during the booking.

## 4. Booking

An authorised employee requests a vehicle by entering the purpose, the start time and the end time of the trip in the booking register.
The Fleet Manager approves or refuses each request before the requested start time.
Where the Fleet Manager would otherwise approve two requests that overlap for one vehicle, the Fleet Manager approves the one entered first and refuses the other.
No employee takes a vehicle without an approved booking.
At the end of the booking, the driver returns the vehicle to the Organisation's car park, enters the mileage in the mileage log and hands the keys to the Fleet Manager.

## 5. Private use

A vehicle is used only for the work of the Organisation.
Private use of a vehicle is not permitted, and the Fleet Manager refuses every request made for a private purpose.
The Fleet Manager may approve a private trip at a weekend, and the driver then pays for its fuel personally.

## 6. Fuel and costs

Each vehicle carries a fuel card, and the driver pays for fuel with that card.
Where a station does not accept the fuel card, the driver pays for the fuel personally and keeps the receipt.
The driver also pays personally for road tolls and parking fees incurred on the trip and keeps the receipts.
The driver hands the fuel, toll and parking receipts to the Finance Officer, who reimburses only the amounts supported by a receipt, within ten working days of receiving it.

## 7. The driver's checks

Before each trip, the driver checks the tyres, the lights, the fuel level, the bodywork and the presence of the vehicle's documents.
The driver who finds a defect, damage or a missing document reports it to the Fleet Manager before driving.
The Fleet Manager decides whether the vehicle may be used, and the driver does not drive it until the Fleet Manager has decided.
A record of each reported defect is kept with the vehicle's documents until the defect is repaired.

## 8. Accidents

After an accident, the driver stops, makes the scene safe and calls the emergency services if any person is injured.
The driver records the place, the time, the other parties and the damage, and does not admit liability.
The driver reports the accident to the Fleet Manager on the day of the accident.
Where the driver is unable to make this report, the driver's line manager makes it on the day the line manager learns of the accident.
The Fleet Manager reports the accident to the insurer and to the Operations Director within two working days of receiving the report.

## 9. Fines

The Organisation pays every fine that relates to a vehicle's registration, insurance or periodic inspection.
Any other fine is paid by the employee who had taken the vehicle and not yet handed back its keys when the offence occurred, or otherwise by the Organisation.
Where a notice of an employee's fine reaches the Organisation, the Fleet Manager identifies that employee and passes the notice on within three working days.
That employee pays the fine by the date stated in the notice.
