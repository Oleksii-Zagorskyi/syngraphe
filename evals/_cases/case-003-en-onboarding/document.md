# Standard Operating Procedure: Onboarding a New Employee

## 1. Scope

This procedure applies to every new employee of the Organisation.
A new employee is a person who has signed an employment contract with the Organisation.

## 2. Terms

A working day is a day on which the office of the Organisation is open.
The first day is the working day on which the new employee starts work under the employment contract.
The first week is the five working days that begin with the first day.
The probation period is the 90 calendar days that begin with the first day.
The equipment is one laptop with its charger, one headset and one building access badge.
The accounts are one email account, one document store account and one timekeeping account.
The leaving day is the final day on which an employee who leaves works for the Organisation.

## 3. Roles

The HR Officer coordinates this procedure and keeps the onboarding record of each new employee.
The IT Technician prepares, issues and recovers the equipment and creates, disables and deletes the accounts.
The line manager is the person to whom the new employee reports under the employment contract.
When the line manager is absent, the line manager's own manager performs the steps that this procedure assigns to the line manager.
When the HR Officer is absent, the Head of HR performs the steps that this procedure assigns to the HR Officer.
The Head of HR owns this procedure and reviews it once every calendar year.

## 4. Before the first day

The HR Officer sets the first day no earlier than ten working days after the contract is signed.
The HR Officer sends an onboarding request to the IT Technician and to the line manager within two working days after the contract is signed.
The IT Technician prepares the equipment and creates the accounts no later than two working days before the first day.
The line manager prepares a written plan for the first week no later than two working days before the first day.
The HR Officer sends the new employee the time and place of arrival no later than three working days before the first day.

## 5. On the first day

The new employee arrives at the office of the Organisation at the time the HR Officer has given.
The HR Officer checks the identity document of the new employee and opens the onboarding record.
The IT Technician hands the equipment to the new employee and gives the sign-in details for the accounts.
The line manager introduces the new employee to the team and explains the plan for the first week.

## 6. In the first week

The new employee completes the information security course and the workplace safety course by the end of the first week.
The line manager gives the new employee written objectives for the probation period by the end of the first week.

## 7. Probation review

The line manager holds a probation review meeting with the new employee no later than day 75 of the probation period.
At the meeting the line manager assesses the work of the new employee against the written objectives.
The line manager decides at the meeting either to confirm the employment or to end it, and sends the decision in writing to the HR Officer on the day of the meeting.
A decision to end the employment names the leaving day, which is no later than the last day of the probation period.
The HR Officer informs the new employee of the decision in writing within two working days after receiving it and enters it in the onboarding record.

## 8. Leaving during the probation period

This section applies when the leaving day of a new employee falls within the probation period, whatever the reason for leaving.
The line manager informs the HR Officer and the IT Technician of the leaving day on the day the line manager sets or learns it.
The employee returns all the equipment to the IT Technician on or before the leaving day.
The content of the accounts is archived before the accounts are deleted.
The IT Technician disables the accounts at the end of the leaving day and deletes them 30 calendar days later.
