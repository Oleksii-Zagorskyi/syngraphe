# Office Closing Checklist

## Purpose and scope

This checklist governs the closing of the Organisation's office on every day, including weekends and public holidays, on which at least one person has entered it.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday.
The closing person is the person who must close the office under the section "Who closes the office" or the section "Weekends and public holidays".

## Who closes the office

On a working day the closing person is the duty officer named for that day on the closing rota.
The Office Manager publishes the closing rota for each month no later than the last working day of the month before.
Neither the Office Manager nor the Head of Operations is ever the duty officer.
If the duty officer has not come in that day, or leaves before the others, each person checks every room before leaving, and the person who finds nobody else is the closing person.
On a day when the Office Manager is on leave or off sick, the Head of Operations takes the Office Manager's place in every rule of this checklist, as actor and as receiver of a report.
Every person other than the duty officer leaves the office by 19:00 on a working day.
The closing person starts the closing steps as soon as every other person has left.

## Weekends and public holidays

On a weekend or a public holiday each person who enters the office writes the time of entry in the closing log, and writes the time of leaving when leaving.
Before leaving, each person checks the closing log and every room to find out whether anyone else is still in the office.
The person who leaves when nobody else remains is the closing person.
A person whom the Office Manager allows to stay past 19:00 on a working day closes the office as on a weekend.

## Closing steps

The closing person carries out the steps below in the order written.

- Windows: the closing person closes and latches every window and checks that the rear door is bolted.
- Lights: the closing person switches off the lights in every room except the entrance hall.
- Equipment: the closing person switches off the printers and every computer that does not carry a "Leave on" label, and leaves the server cabinet running.
- Kitchen: the closing person switches off the kettle and the coffee machine, closes the refrigerator door and leaves the refrigerator running.
- Alarm: the closing person sets the alarm at the panel in the entrance hall and confirms that the panel shows "Armed".
- Doors: the closing person switches off the entrance hall light, leaves through the main door within 60 seconds of setting the alarm and locks both its locks.
- Keys: the closing person puts the office key in the key safe beside the main door and scrambles the code dial.
- Closing log: the closing person writes the date, the time of closing and their own role in the closing log, which is kept in the key safe, and signs the entry.

## If a step cannot be completed

This section applies to each step, other than the alarm step and the doors step, that cannot be completed.
The closing person completes the remaining steps, describes the fault in the closing log and reports it to the Office Manager by message before leaving the building.
The Office Manager orders the repair on the next working day.

## If the alarm will not set

This section applies to the alarm step when the panel does not show "Armed" after two attempts.
The closing person stays in the office and phones the monitoring service once, on the number printed on the panel.
If the monitoring service sets the alarm remotely during that call, the closing person continues with the doors step.
In every other case, including an unanswered call, the closing person completes the doors, keys and closing log steps with the alarm not set and writes "alarm not set" in the closing log.
In both cases the closing person reports the alarm fault to the Office Manager by message before leaving the building.
The Office Manager orders the repair of the alarm on the next working day.
