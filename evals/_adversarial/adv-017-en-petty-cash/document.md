# Petty Cash Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules for the single petty cash fund that the Organisation keeps at its head office.
In this policy, "the fund" means that petty cash fund and nothing else.
This policy applies to every employee who requests, approves, pays or checks a payment from the fund.
A "working day" is a day from Monday to Friday on which the head office is open.

## 2. Roles

The Chief Executive approves this policy and each change to it.
The Finance Director reviews this policy once in every calendar year and appoints the Custodian and the Deputy Custodian in writing.
The Custodian holds the fund, makes payments from it and keeps the petty cash register.
The Deputy Custodian does everything this policy requires of the Custodian on each working day when the Custodian is absent.
The Chief Accountant reconciles the fund and approves its replenishment.
On each working day when the Chief Accountant is absent, the Finance Director does everything this policy requires of the Chief Accountant instead of the Chief Accountant.
A Department Head approves the payment requests of the employees of that department.
The Finance Director approves the payment request of a Department Head.
The Finance Director, the Chief Executive, the Chief Accountant, the Custodian and the Deputy Custodian must not request a payment from the fund.

## 3. Fund limit

The fund limit is 12,000 florins.
The cash in the fund and the receipts paid since the last replenishment must together equal the fund limit at all times.

## 4. Permitted payments

The Custodian pays from the fund only to reimburse an employee for a purchase that the employee has already made for the Organisation.
The fund is used only for the following purchases.

- Office consumables bought for immediate use.
- Postage and courier fees.
- Local taxi and public transport fares for an errand of the Organisation.
- Minor repairs to office equipment.

A purchase of any other kind is not paid from the fund.
The payment limit is 800 florins for one purchase.
A purchase of 800 florins or less may be paid from the fund.
A purchase above 800 florins is not paid from the fund, in whole or in part.
An employee must not divide one purchase into several receipts or several requests.
An employee whose purchase is not paid from the fund may claim it from the Chief Accountant by bank transfer.

## 5. Making a payment

The employee fills in a payment request and attaches the original receipt for the purchase.
The employee submits the request to the approver named in section 2.
The approver signs the request or refuses it in writing within two working days.
The Custodian pays a request only when all of the following are true.

- The request carries the signature of the approver named in section 2.
- The original receipt is attached and shows the amount requested.
- The amount requested is 1,000 florins or less.
- The purchase is of a kind listed in section 4.

The Custodian returns any other request to the employee unpaid, with the reason in writing.
The Custodian records each payment in the petty cash register on the day of payment, and the employee signs the register entry.
When the cash in the fund is less than the amount of a valid request, the Custodian holds the request and pays it on the first working day after replenishment.
When both the Custodian and the Deputy Custodian are absent, no payment is made from the fund until one of them returns.

## 6. Custody and handover

The Custodian stores and counts the cash as set out in the Cash-Handling Procedure.
The fund for travel advances is kept in the same safe, and the Custodian counts it at each handover.
When the Custodian hands the fund to the Deputy Custodian or receives it back, both count the cash together and sign the count in the petty cash register.
The Chief Accountant witnesses each handover and signs the same entry.

## 7. Reconciliation

The Chief Accountant reconciles the fund once a week, on the last working day of the week, in the presence of the Custodian.
In the reconciliation the Chief Accountant counts the cash, adds the receipts paid since the last replenishment and compares the total with the fund limit.
The Chief Accountant and the Custodian both sign the result in the petty cash register.
The person who performs the reconciliation reports any difference, whether a shortage or a surplus, to the Chief Executive in writing on the same working day.
The Chief Executive decides in writing within five working days how the difference is settled.

## 8. Replenishment

When the cash in the fund falls below 3,000 florins, the Custodian submits a replenishment request with the paid receipts to the Chief Accountant on the same working day.
When the cash in the fund is 3,000 florins or more, the Custodian does not request replenishment.
The Chief Accountant checks the receipts against the petty cash register and approves the request within one working day.
The replenishment amount equals the total of the paid receipts, so that the cash in the fund returns to the fund limit.
The Custodian withdraws the approved amount from the bank on the next working day.
