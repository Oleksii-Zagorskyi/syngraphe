# Cash-Handling Procedure

## Purpose and scope

This procedure governs receiving, holding, counting and handing over cash at the cash desk of the Organisation.
The cash desk receives payments in cash and pays out no cash other than change.
Moving cash from the safe to the bank is outside this procedure.

## Terms

- The drawer is the locked drawer at the cash desk in which the cashier on shift holds cash.
- The safe is the locked steel cabinet in the back office.
- The desk limit is 4,000 crowns of cash in the drawer.
- The float is 500 crowns issued from the safe to the drawer for giving change.
- The cash ledger is the bound book that records every payment, every transfer between the drawer and the safe, and the balance of the drawer and of the safe.
- A discrepancy is any difference, of any amount, between an agreed counted amount and the balance in the cash ledger.

## Roles and working hours

The cash desk is open every calendar day from 08:00 to 20:00.
The morning shift runs from 08:00 to 14:00, and the afternoon shift runs from 14:00 to 20:00.
The Finance Controller assigns one cashier and one Shift Supervisor to each shift.
When an assigned person is absent, the Finance Controller assigns a replacement in the same role before the shift starts.
The cashier on shift holds the only key to the drawer.
The Shift Supervisor on shift holds the only key to the safe.
After the daily count the afternoon cashier and the afternoon Shift Supervisor each keep their key and give it to the person in the same role on the next morning shift before the float is issued.
The cashier accepts no cash during a hand-over or the daily count.

## Opening the cash desk

Before 08:00 the morning Shift Supervisor takes the float from the safe and counts it together with the morning cashier.
The morning cashier places the float in the drawer.
The morning cashier records the transfer in the cash ledger, and the cashier and the Shift Supervisor both sign the entry.

## Receiving payments

The cashier counts each payment in front of the payer and places the cash in the drawer.
The cashier records each payment in the cash ledger at once and gives the payer a signed slip.

## Desk limit

After each payment the cashier compares the drawer balance in the cash ledger with the desk limit.
When the drawer balance is 4,000 crowns or less, the cashier keeps the cash in the drawer.
When the drawer balance is more than 4,000 crowns, the cashier transfers all cash above 1,000 crowns to the safe within 30 minutes of the payment.
For each transfer the cashier and the Shift Supervisor count the cash together, and the Shift Supervisor locks it in the safe.
The cashier records the transfer in the cash ledger, and the cashier and the Shift Supervisor both sign the entry.

## Hand-over between shifts

At 14:00 the morning cashier hands the drawer over to the afternoon cashier.
The morning cashier completes any transfer due under the section "Desk limit" before the hand-over begins.
The morning cashier counts the cash in the drawer, and the afternoon cashier recounts it.
When the two counts differ, the two cashiers count the cash together once more, and the result of that joint count is the agreed counted amount.
When the two counts are equal, that amount is the agreed counted amount.
The morning cashier records the agreed counted amount in the cash ledger, and both cashiers sign the entry.
The morning cashier then gives the drawer key to the afternoon cashier, and the morning Shift Supervisor gives the safe key to the afternoon Shift Supervisor.
The safe is not counted at the hand-over.

## Daily count

At 20:00 the daily count takes place instead of a hand-over.
The afternoon cashier counts the cash in the drawer, and the afternoon Shift Supervisor recounts it.
The afternoon Shift Supervisor counts the cash in the safe, and the afternoon cashier recounts it.
The agreed counted amount of the drawer and of the safe is set by the same rule as in the section "Hand-over between shifts", with the afternoon cashier and the afternoon Shift Supervisor as the two counters.
The afternoon cashier records both agreed counted amounts in the cash ledger, and the cashier and the Shift Supervisor both sign the entry.
The afternoon Shift Supervisor then locks all cash from the drawer in the safe, and the afternoon cashier records this transfer in the cash ledger.

## Discrepancies

The cashier who recorded an agreed counted amount reports any discrepancy to the Shift Supervisor of that cashier's shift immediately.
That Shift Supervisor writes a discrepancy report on the same day and sends it to the Finance Controller within 24 hours of the count.
No cashier and no Shift Supervisor adds cash to the drawer or the safe, or removes cash from them, to remove a discrepancy.
The Finance Controller investigates each discrepancy and records the outcome in the cash ledger within five calendar days of receiving the report.
Only the Finance Controller corrects a balance in the cash ledger.

## Records

The Finance Controller keeps each completed cash ledger and each discrepancy report for six years.
