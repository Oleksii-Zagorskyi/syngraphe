# Policy on Keeping Video Recordings from Cameras

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets how long the Organisation keeps video recordings from its cameras, who may view them, and how a person asks to view or receive one.
It applies to every recording stored by the recording system of the Organisation, from the moment the recording is stored until it is deleted.

## Terms

A recording is video that a camera of the Organisation has stored in the recording system.
A hold is a mark in the recording system that stops the deletion of one recording.
A request is a written application to view a recording or to receive a copy.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday on which the head office of the Organisation is open.

## Retention period

The Organisation keeps each recording for 30 calendar days, counted from the day after the day on which the recording was made.
The recording system deletes each recording automatically when its 30 days end, except a recording under a hold.
The Facilities Manager checks on the first working day of each week that the automatic deletion has worked, and deletes by hand on that day any recording that the system should have deleted and did not.

## Holds

The Security Officer places a hold on a recording, on the same day, in each of two situations.

- The Security Officer registers a request that concerns the recording.
- The Security Officer enters in the incident register an incident that the recording shows.

The Data Protection Officer checks every hold on the first working day of each month.
The Data Protection Officer releases a hold in writing when no open request and no open incident concerns the recording.
A request is open until the requester has received a refusal, has viewed the recording, or has received the copy.
An incident is open until the Security Officer marks it closed in the incident register.
When a hold is released on a recording whose 30 days have already ended, the Facilities Manager deletes the recording within 3 working days of the release.
Every other released recording is deleted as the section "Retention period" states.

## Who may view recordings

Three roles may view a recording without a request.

- The Security Officer may view a recording only to examine an incident entered in the incident register.
- The Facilities Manager may view a recording only to check that a camera or the recording system works.
- The Data Protection Officer may view a recording only to decide a request that concerns it.

Every other person, and each of these three roles for any other purpose, may view a recording only after an approved request.
The Chief Executive has no right to view recordings and submits no requests, even when the Chief Executive appears in a recording.
Each person who views a recording without a request writes in the viewing log, on the same day, the date, the recording and the reason.
The Security Officer writes the same details in the viewing log for each viewing that follows an approved request.
The Data Protection Officer reads the viewing log on the first working day of each month.
The Chief Executive reads, in the same week, the entries that record viewings by the Data Protection Officer.

## Request procedure

Any member of staff, any person who appears in a recording, and any public authority may submit a request.
The Organisation keeps recordings for 45 calendar days, and a request may concern any of them.
The requester sends the request to the Security Officer and states the date, the time, the camera location, the purpose, and whether a viewing or a copy is wanted.
The Security Officer registers the request in the request register within 1 working day of receiving it.
The Security Officer also registers there a camera repair request from the Facilities Manager.
If the recording has already been deleted, the Security Officer tells the requester so in writing within the same working day and closes the request, instead of passing it on.
Otherwise the Security Officer passes the request to the Data Protection Officer on the day of registration.
The Data Protection Officer decides the request within 5 working days of its registration.
The Chief Executive decides, within the same time, a request submitted by the Data Protection Officer, instead of the Data Protection Officer.
The deciding role approves a request only when it serves one of the following purposes.

- The requester appears in the recording and wants to see or receive their own image.
- The requester is the member of staff assigned in the incident register to investigate an incident that the recording shows.
- The requester is a public authority and encloses a written demand that the law obliges the Organisation to obey.

The deciding role refuses every other request.
The deciding role sends the decision and its reasons to the requester and to the Security Officer in writing on the day of the decision.
After an approval, the Security Officer arranges the viewing in the security room, or hands over the copy, within 5 working days of the decision.
The Security Officer records each copy in the request register and keeps no further copy outside the recording system.
