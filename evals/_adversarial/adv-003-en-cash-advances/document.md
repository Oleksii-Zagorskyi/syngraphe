# Policy on Cash Advances for Business Expenses

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how the Organisation pays money to an Employee before the Employee spends it on the Organisation's business.
It applies to every Employee of the Organisation except the Managing Director, who receives no advances.

## 2. Terms

An advance is a sum that the Organisation transfers to an Employee's salary account for one stated business purpose.
The Approver is the role to which the requesting Employee reports according to the Organisation's staff register.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday on which the Organisation's office is open.
An open advance is an advance that has been paid and has not yet been closed under section 5, 7 or 8.

## 3. Maximum advance

One advance may be any amount up to and including 12,000 florins, and the Approver must refuse a request for more.
An Employee may hold only one open advance at a time, and the Approver must refuse a request from an Employee who holds one.

## 4. Request and approval

The Employee fills in the Advance Request Form, whose fields are listed in Appendix A, and sends it to the Approver.
The Approver approves or refuses the request on the form within 2 working days after receiving it, and states the reason for a refusal.
On the day of the decision, the Approver returns a refused form to the Employee and sends an approved form to the Cashier.

## 5. Payment and use

The Cashier transfers the advance to the Employee's salary account within 2 working days after receiving the approved form.
For an advance of more than 8,000 florins, the Finance Director is the Approver and countersigns the approved form before the Cashier makes the transfer.
When the Employee who is to receive any transfer under this policy is the Cashier, the Chief Accountant makes that transfer instead of the Cashier.
The Employee spends the advance only on the purpose stated in the approved form and only on or before the end date stated in it.
If the purpose is cancelled before any of the advance is spent, the Employee returns the whole advance to the Organisation's bank account within 2 working days after the cancellation and informs the Chief Accountant in writing on the day of the return.
The Chief Accountant closes that advance on the day of the return, and sections 6 and 7 do not apply to it.
If the purpose is cancelled after part of the advance is spent, sections 6 and 7 apply, and the day of the cancellation replaces the end date.

## 6. Expense report

The Employee submits an expense report to the Chief Accountant within 5 working days after the end date stated in the approved form.
The expense report lists each expense with its date, amount and receipt.
The Chief Accountant checks the report and notifies the Employee and the Approver of the result in writing within 3 working days after receiving it.
For an advance paid to the Chief Accountant, the Finance Director performs every duty that sections 5 to 8 give to the Chief Accountant.
The Chief Accountant accepts an expense only if all of the following are true.

- The expense serves the purpose stated in the approved form.
- The expense was paid on or before the end date stated in the approved form.
- A receipt for the expense is attached to the report.

The Chief Accountant rejects every other expense, and a rejected expense is not counted as spending of the advance.

## 7. Settlement and closing

If the accepted expenses are less than the advance, the Employee returns the difference to the Organisation's bank account within 3 working days after the notification under section 6.
If the accepted expenses are more than the advance, the Approver decides in writing, within 2 working days after the notification, whether the Organisation reimburses the difference.
The Cashier transfers an approved reimbursement to the Employee within 5 working days after the Approver's decision, and the Employee bears a difference that the Approver declines to reimburse.
If the accepted expenses equal the advance, no payment is due from either side.
The Chief Accountant closes the advance on the day when no payment remains due under this section.

## 8. Late report or late return

An expense report is late only when it reaches the Chief Accountant more than 10 working days after the end date stated in the approved form.
If the Employee does not submit the expense report within the time limit in section 6, the Chief Accountant deducts the whole advance from the Employee's next salary payment.
If the Employee does not return a sum within the time limit in section 5 or section 7, the Chief Accountant deducts that sum from the Employee's next salary payment.
The Chief Accountant closes the advance on the day of a deduction.
The Chief Accountant does not check an expense report submitted after the time limit in section 6, and the Organisation reimburses no expense listed in it.

## 9. Ownership of this policy

The Finance Director keeps this policy current and reviews it once every calendar year.
The Managing Director approves every change to this policy.

## Appendix A. Fields of the Advance Request Form

- Employee's full name and position.
- Approver's position.
- Business purpose of the advance.
- Amount requested in florins.
- Start date and end date of the purpose.
- Employee's consent to the deductions described in section 8.
- Employee's signature and date of the request.
- Approver's decision, reason for a refusal, signature and date.
