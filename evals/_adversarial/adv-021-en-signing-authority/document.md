# Policy on Delegating Signing Authority

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy states who may sign a commitment on behalf of the Organisation and how that authority is delegated.
It applies to every commitment, to every amendment of a commitment, and to every document that ends a commitment.

## 2. Definitions

A commitment is a contract, order or other document that binds the Organisation to pay money to an outside party or entitles it to receive money from one.
The value of a commitment is the total amount payable or receivable under it over its full duration, in euros, without taxes.
A band is one of the four ranges of value set out in Appendix A.
A delegation is a written grant of signing authority made by the Chief Executive under section 4.
A delegate is an employee who holds a delegation that is in force.
A signatory is a person who has authority under this policy to sign a given commitment.

## 3. Original authority

The Board gives the Chief Executive authority to sign commitments in Bands 1, 2 and 3.
The Chief Executive signs a commitment in Band 4 only after the Board has approved that commitment by resolution.
The Chair of the Board exercises every power and duty of the Chief Executive under this policy while the office of Chief Executive is vacant, during an absence that the Chief Executive has notified to the Company Secretary in writing, and while a Board resolution declaring the Chief Executive unable to act is in force.

## 4. Granting a delegation

The Chief Executive may delegate authority to sign commitments in Bands 1, 2 and 3 to an employee of the Organisation.
The Chief Executive may not delegate authority to sign commitments in Band 4.
The Chief Executive grants each delegation in writing and sends it to the Company Secretary.
The Company Secretary countersigns each delegation as its second signatory.
Each delegation states the following.

- It states the name of the delegate and the post that the delegate holds.
- It states the highest band that the delegate may sign.
- It states the start date and the end date.

A delegate may sign commitments in the highest band stated in the delegation and in every lower band.
A delegate may not pass the delegated authority to another person.

## 5. Maximum term

The end date of a delegation is no later than 12 months after its start date.
The Company Secretary returns to the Chief Executive, without entering it in the register, a delegation that has no end date or whose end date is later than that limit.
To continue the authority of a delegate after the end date, the Chief Executive grants a new delegation under section 4.

## 6. Register of delegations

The Company Secretary keeps the register of delegations and makes it available to all employees.
The Company Secretary enters each delegation that meets sections 4 and 5 in the register within one working day of receiving it.
A delegation comes into force at the start of its start date or at the time it is entered in the register, whichever is later.
A delegation may run for up to 18 months from its start date.
The Company Secretary reports the delegations in force to the Board at the end of each quarter.

## 7. Ending a delegation

A delegation stays in force until the end of the earliest of the following days and is not in force after that day.

- The first day is its end date.
- The second day is the day the Company Secretary records in the register a written revocation by the Chief Executive.
- The third day is the last day on which the delegate holds the post stated in the delegation.

The Company Secretary records a revocation on the day of receiving it.
The Head of Human Resources notifies the Company Secretary of the last day of a delegate in the stated post no later than that day, and the Company Secretary records it in the register within one working day.

## 8. Determining the band

Before signing, the signatory determines the value of the commitment and its band under Appendix A.
The band of an amendment is the band of the total value of the commitment after the amendment.
The band of a document that ends a commitment is the band of the commitment that it ends.
A commitment whose value cannot be calculated on the day of signing is in Band 4.
The Finance Director publishes a euro conversion rate for each other currency on the first working day of each month.
The signatory converts a value stated in another currency at the rate published for the month of signing.
Before signing, a delegate confirms in the register that the delegation is in force and covers the band.

## 9. Signing outside authority

An employee who learns that a commitment was signed by a person who was not a signatory for it reports this to the Company Secretary on the same working day.
The Company Secretary reports it to the Chair of the Board within two working days.
The Board decides at its next meeting whether the Organisation accepts or disputes the commitment.

## Appendix A. Value bands

- Band 1 covers a value up to and including 10,000 euros.
- Band 2 covers a value above 10,000 euros, up to and including 100,000 euros.
- Band 3 covers a value above 100,000 euros, up to and including 1,000,000 euros.
- Band 4 covers a value above 1,000,000 euros.
