# Policy on Declaring Conflicts of Interest

## Purpose and scope

This policy governs the declaring of conflicts of interest by every staff member and every Board member of the Organisation, who are together called covered persons.
A staff member is any person employed by the Organisation, including the Director.
A Board member is any member of the Supervisory Board, including the Chair.

## Definitions

A private interest is a financial or personal benefit that a covered person or a close person of that covered person may gain or lose.
A close person is a spouse, a partner, a parent, a child, a sibling or any person who shares the household of the covered person.
A matter is the decision, contract or task of the Organisation that a private interest relates to.
A conflict of interest is a situation in which a private interest of a covered person could affect, or could reasonably appear to affect, how that person acts in a matter.
A declarant is a covered person who has submitted a declaration form.
A working day is any day from Monday to Friday, except a public holiday that the Organisation observes.

## Duty to declare

A covered person declares every conflict of interest that concerns them, and declares a situation also when unsure whether it is a conflict of interest.
A covered person takes no part in the matter from the moment they become aware of the conflict of interest until a decision is made under the section "Who decides".

## Declaration form

A covered person declares by completing form CI-1 and sending it to the register mailbox.
When the Board Secretary is on leave, the Director appoints another staff member to act as Board Secretary under this policy.
Form CI-1 holds the following.

- The form states the name and role of the covered person.
- The form describes the matter and the private interest, and names the person who holds that private interest.
- The form states the date on which the covered person became aware of the conflict of interest.

## Time limit for a declaration

A covered person submits form CI-1 within 5 working days after the day on which they became aware of the conflict of interest.

## Who decides

The Board Secretary forwards each form to the decision-maker within 1 working day after the day it reaches the register mailbox.

- The Compliance Officer decides on a declaration by a staff member other than the Compliance Officer and the Director.
- The Chair decides on a declaration by the Compliance Officer or by the Director.
- The Board decides on a declaration by a Board member.

A decision-maker who holds a private interest in the same matter, or who is a close person of the declarant, returns the form to the Board Secretary within 1 working day after receiving it.
When the Compliance Officer returns a form, the Board Secretary forwards it on the same day to the Chair, who decides instead of the Compliance Officer.
When the Chair returns a form, the Board Secretary forwards it on the same day to the Board, which decides instead of the Chair.

A decision-maker other than the Board decides within 10 working days after the day on which they receive the form.
The decision-maker chooses one of two outcomes: the declarant continues in the matter, or the declarant withdraws from the matter.
The decision-maker sends the decision, with written reasons, to the declarant and to the Board Secretary on the day of the decision.

When the Board decides, the Board Secretary opens a written vote of the eligible Board members on the day the form is forwarded.
An eligible Board member is a Board member who is not the declarant, holds no private interest in the same matter and is not a close person of the declarant.
Each eligible Board member votes for one of the two outcomes before the vote closes at the end of the tenth working day after the day it opens.
The declarant continues in the matter only if at least two votes are cast and more than half of the votes cast are for that outcome.
In every other result of the vote, the declarant withdraws from the matter.
The Board Secretary tells the declarant the result on the working day after the day the vote closes.

## Effect of withdrawal

A Board member who withdraws does not attend the discussion of the matter and does not vote on it.
Each withdrawal is recorded in the minutes of every meeting at which the matter is discussed.
When a staff member other than the Director withdraws, the Director assigns the matter to another staff member who holds no private interest in it.
When the Director withdraws, the Chair assigns the matter to a staff member who holds no private interest in it.

## Register

The Board Secretary keeps the register of conflicts of interest and enters each form in it within 2 working days after the day it reaches the register mailbox.
The Board Secretary forwards each form to the decision-maker with its register entry within 3 working days after the day it reaches the register mailbox.
The Board Secretary adds the decision-maker, the outcome and its date to the entry within 2 working days after the day of the decision or the day the vote closes.
The Board Secretary keeps each entry for 6 years after the date of the outcome and deletes it in the following month.
