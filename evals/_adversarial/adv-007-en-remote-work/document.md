# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets the rules under which employees of the Organisation work remotely.
It applies to every employee who holds an employment contract with the Organisation.
It does not apply to the Executive Director, whose working arrangements the Board sets.

## Definitions

A working day is a day from Monday to Friday that is not a public holiday.
A week runs from Monday to Sunday.
Every employment contract of the Organisation sets work on working days only.
A remote day is a working day on which the employee performs any part of their duties from the home address recorded in the People System.
An office day is a working day on which the employee performs all their duties at the Organisation's premises, at a client's premises or on a business trip.
An employee must not perform duties from any other place.
The remote kit is a headset, a privacy screen filter and a spare laptop charger.

## Eligibility

An employee is eligible for remote work when all of the following conditions are met.

- The employee has completed the probation period set in their contract, or the contract sets none.
- The employee's role is marked as remote-capable in the Role Register.
- The employee has no active written warning recorded in the People System.

The People Team maintains the Role Register, and the Executive Director approves each change to it.
An employee who holds no approval works every working day as an office day.

## Number of remote days

An employee who holds an approval and is contracted to work five days a week may take up to two remote days in a week.
An employee who holds an approval and is contracted to work fewer than five days a week may take up to one remote day in a week.
Remote days that the employee does not take in a week do not carry over to a later week.

## Approval

An eligible employee submits a request to their line manager through the People System and names the weekdays requested as remote days.
The line manager checks the eligibility conditions against the People System and the Role Register.
The line manager approves or refuses the request within five working days after the day of receipt and records the decision in the People System.
The line manager states the reason for a refusal in writing.
If the line manager records no decision within that period, the request is treated as refused on the last day of the period.
An approval is valid for twelve months from the day it is recorded.
An approval allows up to three remote days in each week.
To continue remote work after that, the employee submits a new request.
An employee may submit a new request at any time.
The employee takes remote days only on the approved weekdays, except that the line manager may consent in writing, before the day concerned, to a different weekday in a single week.
The line manager may require an employee to attend the Organisation's premises on an approved remote day by giving notice of at least two working days.
With shorter notice, the employee attends only if the employee agrees.

## Appeals

An employee may appeal against a refusal or a withdrawal within ten working days after the day it is recorded or treated as given.
The Head of People decides an appeal, unless the decision appealed against was made by the Head of People or by the Executive Director.
The Executive Director decides an appeal against a decision made by the Head of People, and the Board Chair decides an appeal against a decision made by the Executive Director.
The employee sends the appeal to the role that decides it, and that role decides it within ten working days after the day it receives the appeal.
The Head of People reports to the Executive Director.
The decision on an appeal is final.

## Withdrawal

The People Team notifies the line manager within two working days when an employee stops meeting an eligibility condition.
The line manager then records the withdrawal in the People System, with effect from the next working day.
The employee's access to the remote-access service is closed on the day the withdrawal takes effect.
The line manager may also withdraw an approval for operational reasons by recording written reasons and at least ten working days' notice in the People System.

## Equipment

The employee uses only the laptop issued by the Organisation for work on a remote day.
The IT Team issues the remote kit to the employee before the first remote day under an approval.
The employee provides the internet connection and furniture at the home address at their own cost.
The employee reports loss, theft or damage of the laptop or the remote kit to the IT Team no later than the end of the next working day.
The IT Team repairs or replaces the item within five working days after the report, and the Organisation bears the cost.
Until the laptop is repaired or replaced, the employee works every working day as an office day.
The employee returns the remote kit to the IT Team within five working days after the employee stops holding an approval.
An employee whose employment ends returns the remote kit on their last working day instead.

## Ownership and review

The Head of People owns this policy and reviews it every twelve months.
The Executive Director approves each change to this policy.
