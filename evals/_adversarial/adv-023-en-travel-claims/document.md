# Staff Travel Expense Claims Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how an employee of the Organisation recovers travel costs paid from the employee's own money.
It applies to every employee of the Organisation, including the Executive Director.
It does not cover costs that the Organisation pays directly to a supplier, and it does not cover cash advances.

## Terms

A trip is a journey on the Organisation's business that the approver authorised in writing before the journey began.
A journey without that written authorisation is not a trip, and the Organisation reimburses no cost of it.
The claimant is the employee who made the trip and asks for reimbursement.
The approver is the claimant's line manager.
The approver of the Executive Director's trips and claims is the Chair of the Board.
A working day is a day on which the Organisation's head office is open.

## Costs that may be claimed

The claimant may claim the following costs of a trip.

- Public transport fares in standard class, at the price paid.
- Lodging, up to and including 120 crowns for each night.
- Meals, up to and including 30 crowns for each calendar day of the trip.
- Parking charges and road tolls, at the price paid.
- Use of a private vehicle, at the mileage rate set in the section "Mileage rate".

The Organisation does not reimburse the part of a lodging or meal cost that is above its limit.
The Finance Officer converts a cost paid in another currency into crowns at the rate that the Organisation's bank published for the date on the receipt.

## Mileage rate

The mileage rate is 0.45 crowns for each kilometre driven on the trip in a private vehicle.
A trip to a client's site that the claimant makes without written authorisation is paid at the same mileage rate.
The claimant records the odometer reading at the start and at the end of the trip and enters both readings on the claim form.
The mileage rate covers fuel, wear, insurance and maintenance, so the claimant does not claim those costs separately.

## Receipts

The claimant attaches a receipt for every cost claimed, except mileage.
If a receipt is lost and the cost is 20 crowns or less, the claimant attaches a signed declaration of the cost instead of the receipt.
The Organisation does not reimburse a cost above 20 crowns, other than mileage, that has no receipt.

## Claim form

The claimant completes one Travel Claim Form TC-1 for each trip.
On the form the claimant states the dates and purpose of the trip, each cost with its date and amount, the odometer readings where mileage is claimed, and the total.
The claimant signs the form and attaches the written authorisation of the trip.

## Time limit for a claim

The claimant submits the form to the Finance Officer no later than 30 calendar days after the last day of the trip.
The Finance Officer refuses in writing a form first submitted after that time limit, and the Organisation does not reimburse it.

## Checking and approval

A form that reaches the Finance Officer within 60 calendar days after the last day of the trip is in time.
Within 5 working days after receiving the form, the Finance Officer checks that it is complete, that the receipts match the costs and that the limits and the mileage rate are applied correctly.
If the form passes these checks, the Finance Officer sends it to the approver on the same day.
If the form fails a check, the Finance Officer returns it to the claimant with written reasons.
The claimant may resubmit a returned form within 10 working days after the return, and the 30-day time limit does not apply to a resubmission.
A returned form that is not resubmitted within those 10 working days lapses, and the Organisation does not reimburse it.

Within 5 working days after receiving the form, the approver decides on each cost.
The approver approves a cost that was incurred for the trip and refuses any other cost with written reasons.
The approver sends the decision to the Finance Officer and to the claimant on the day it is made.
The Organisation does not reimburse a refused cost.

When the claimant is the Finance Officer, the Finance Manager carries out the Finance Officer's duties under this policy for that claim instead of the Finance Officer.

## Reimbursement deadline

The Finance Officer pays the approved costs to the claimant's salary bank account within 10 working days after receiving the approver's decision.

## Records

The Finance Officer keeps each form, its attachments and any approver's decision on it for 6 years after the day on which the form was first submitted.
