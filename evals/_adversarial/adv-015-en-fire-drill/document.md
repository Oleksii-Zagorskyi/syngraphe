# Standard Operating Procedure: Fire Evacuation Drill

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how the Organisation runs a fire evacuation drill in its office building.
The building has three floors: the ground floor, the first floor and the second floor.
An occupant is any person inside the building when the alarm sounds, whether an employee, a contractor or a visitor.
This procedure applies to every occupant.

## 2. Roles

The Safety Manager plans each drill, directs it and writes the report.
The Facilities Director appoints one Fire Warden and one Deputy Fire Warden for each floor.
The Deputy Fire Warden performs every duty of the Fire Warden of the same floor when that Fire Warden is absent.
The Reception Officer keeps the attendance record, which lists every occupant who is in the building on the day.

## 3. Frequency and schedule

The Safety Manager holds two scheduled drills in each calendar year, one between 1 January and 30 June and one between 1 July and 31 December.
The Safety Manager proposes the two dates for the following year by 15 December.
The Facilities Director approves the dates or sets different dates by 31 December.
The Safety Manager tells the Fire Wardens and the Deputy Fire Wardens the date of each drill at least five working days before it.
The Safety Manager postpones a drill if, on the day, an assembly point is blocked or a floor has neither its Fire Warden nor its Deputy Fire Warden.
The Facilities Director sets the new date of a postponed drill, which must fall within 14 calendar days after the original date.
A postponed drill counts for the half of the year in which it was first scheduled.

## 4. Assembly points

Assembly Point A is the north car park.
Assembly Point B is the lawn beside the east gate.
Occupants who are on the ground floor or the first floor when the alarm sounds go to Assembly Point A.
Occupants who are on the second floor when the alarm sounds go to Assembly Point B.
An occupant on the first floor or the second floor who cannot use the stairs goes instead to the refuge point on the stair landing of the floor and waits there with the Fire Warden of that floor.

## 5. Steps of the drill

The steps are carried out in the order listed.

- The Safety Manager confirms that both assembly points are clear and that a Fire Warden or a Deputy Fire Warden is present on each floor.
- The Safety Manager activates the alarm and records the time.
- Every occupant stops work and walks, without using a lift, to the place set in section 4.
- The Reception Officer takes the attendance record and a radio to Assembly Point A.
- Each Fire Warden checks every room of the floor and closes the doors.
- Each Fire Warden then goes to the assembly point of the floor, except that a Fire Warden with an occupant at the refuge point stays there.
- Each Fire Warden reports to the Reception Officer by radio the number of occupants who left the floor, adding the Fire Warden and any occupants at the refuge point.
- The Reception Officer adds the three numbers, compares the sum with the attendance record and tells the Safety Manager the result.
- The Safety Manager records the time at which the Reception Officer received the third number.
- The alarm system is reset before any occupant returns to the building.
- The Safety Manager announces the end of the drill by radio, and only then do the Fire Wardens tell the occupants to return.

If an occupant sees fire or smoke during the drill, the occupant tells the nearest Fire Warden, who tells the Safety Manager by radio.
The Safety Manager then ends the drill, and the Organisation's Fire Emergency Procedure applies instead of this procedure.
A drill ended in this way is a postponed drill under section 3.

## 6. Result of the drill

The evacuation time is the time from the activation of the alarm to the moment the Reception Officer received the third number.
A drill is satisfactory if the evacuation time is six minutes or less and the sum equalled the attendance record.
A drill is unsatisfactory if the evacuation time is more than six minutes or the sum differed from the attendance record.
After an unsatisfactory drill, the Safety Manager holds a repeat drill within 30 calendar days.
A repeat drill is additional to the two scheduled drills and follows every rule of this procedure.

## 7. Report after the drill

The Safety Manager writes the drill report within five working days after the drill.
The report states the date, the evacuation time, the three numbers, the result under section 6 and each weakness observed, with a proposed corrective action for each.
The report records the drill as satisfactory when the evacuation time is eight minutes or less and the sum equalled the attendance record.
The Safety Manager sends the report to the Facilities Director, who approves the report or returns it with comments within five working days after receiving it.
The Safety Manager corrects a returned report and sends it again within three working days, and the Facilities Director reviews it in the same way.
When approving the report, the Facilities Director assigns each corrective action to a role other than the Facilities Director and sets a due date.
The Facilities Director confirms that each corrective action is complete.
