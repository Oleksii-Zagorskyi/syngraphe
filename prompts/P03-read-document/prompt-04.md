Find each problem of the six kinds below in the document.
Report each problem as a separate finding, and give its kind.

Read the document as the reader who obeys it.
This reader knows only the text of the document.
Read the whole document before you report a problem: a different passage can remove it.

## The kinds of problem

- `A01` Contradiction: two passages cannot both be true under any reading. A reader who obeys one passage breaks the other passage.
  - Two passages give two values for the same thing.
  - One passage permits an action, and a different passage forbids it in the same case.
  - A passage does not obey a rule or a definition of the document.
- `A02` Ambiguity: two or more passages, read together, give two careful readers two different actions. Each reader believes that they obey the document.
  - Two rules apply to the same case, and no rule tells which one applies.
  - The reach of an exception or a condition is not clear.
  - A reference can point to two parts of the document.
- `A03` Gap: the scope of the document includes a case, and no rule of the document handles that case.
  - A range of values has an interval that no rule covers.
  - The scope names a type, a role or a place that no rule covers.
  - A passage names two or more places, types or items. A later rule that applies to all of them names only some of them, and no rule handles the others.
  - A step has a rule for one result and no rule for the other result.
  - A procedure gives a stand-in to each of its roles but one. That one role has a duty with a time limit, and the document gives no stand-in to it.
- `A04` Term with two meanings: the document uses one term for two different things, and a reader can act on the wrong meaning.
  - The document defines a term. A passage uses the term where the text near it names a different thing, for example a role with a similar name.
- `A05` Circular authority: a role approves its own act, or two or more roles approve the acts of each other in a circle.
  - Follow the path when the approver is also the requester.
  - Follow the path when the matter is about the approver.
  - Follow the path when a stand-in does the duties of an absent role and approves its own act.
  - Compare each two rules that put one role in place of another role. If role A acts in place of role B for the matters of B, and role B acts in place of role A for the matters of A, then A and B approve the acts of each other. Report this, also when each rule alone is a correct replacement.
  - Compare each rule that puts role B in place of role A with the general rules. If a general rule gives role A the decision on the acts of every person, then A also decides the acts of B, and B decides the acts of A. A and B approve the acts of each other. Report this.
  - A rule that puts one role in place of another role is not an exception that removes the problem. Follow the path through it.
- `A06` Unowned obligation: the document requires an action and assigns no role to do it. Or the document gives a right to a role, and no role is accountable for its use.
  - A right lets a role not do, delay or change a required action. No passage makes the role tell, explain, record or end its use.
  - A step receives a thing, for example a document, a notice or a decision, and no passage tells which role sends it.

## How to choose the kind

Give each finding the one kind that describes it best.
Report one problem once, also when it fits two kinds.
Use the definitions above, not the words that the document uses.

## Do not report these

- A sentence that has two readings only when you read it alone.
- A vague word that has only one reading, for example "soon" or "appropriate".
- A case in which a person does not do what the document requires, or does it late.
- A general rule and an exception that the document marks, for example with "except", "instead" or "unless". This does not apply to `A05`: a role in place of a different role can still approve its own act.
- A general rule and a narrower rule for a special case. The narrower rule applies to its case.
- Two limits of the same kind, if the reader obeys both limits when they obey the stricter limit.
- A passage that a different passage decides, for example a limiting clause or a definition. But report a defined term that a passage uses for a different thing (`A04`).
- A second time limit that starts from a different event.
- The absence of a role, if the document gives no stand-in for any role.
- A request, a notice, a report or an exchange of information. These are not approvals.
- A rule that tells how a value is counted, or a result that the rule itself makes. These are not actions.
- A detail that the reader does not need to act, for example a form, a format or a template.

Quote the passages that the problem uses.
In the explanation, write the case, and what the reader cannot do or does wrongly.
