# History, comparing and undoing

## The History tab

*History* lists a document's versions, newest first: each with its number, what happened — an edit, a reviewer's version, an acceptance, a return, a canonisation — who acted and when, and the freezes and unfreezes between them. A line marks where the accepted text ends: above it is the work not yet accepted.

What you see depends on your groups' rights. *View all history* shows every version your reading right reaches. *View previous canonical versions* shows the editions alone. *Accept* without a history right shows the versions since the last acceptance. Without any of these rights the tab is not shown.

## A version on its own page

*Open* shows a version in a new window, read only: a band across the top names it and links back to the current document. The page has the outline, the attachments as that version listed them, *Export* of that version, and *Compare with* the other versions you may see.

## Comparing two versions

A comparison shows the newer of two versions in full, with its changes against the older marked in place. Attachments cannot be compared: each is listed with its file in both versions and whether it was added, changed — its file, title or file name — removed or unchanged. When the two versions follow each other, *previous* and *next* move the pair one version back or forward, skipping the versions you may not see.

## Returning a run without accepting it

A reviewer who declines the edits waiting since the last acceptance chooses *Return without accepting* on the *Pending edits* tab and gives a reason: one line, required. The return is recorded in the document's own record, as an acceptance is. The edits stay pending; their authors are told, and read the reason in their notifications while they may still read the pending edits — the email names only the event. The document shows *returned* until a new version is sent, and then *awaiting review* again; *In review* leaves it out meanwhile. Nobody may return while the document is reserved.

## Undoing a version or an author's edits

A holder of *accept* can undo, from *History*, one version of the edits since the last acceptance — *Undo this edit* — or everything one author wrote since then — *Undo all of this author's edits*. The undo is typed into your own draft, which opens; nothing is removed from the history, and nothing is committed until you finish. Where a later version, or your draft, changed what the undone edits wrote, that place is left as it stands and listed above the editor, with the wording before the undone edits, what they wrote, and the wording now, for you to edit by hand. A title or a part changed by the undone edits is not undone here.

Finishing with *Accept as* commits your version and accepts the edits; every author whose lines were removed receives a correction notice. *Create version* leaves it pending. While your undo waits in your draft, nobody accepts the edits from the *Pending edits* tab: they are accepted with your *Accept as*. An undo waits while someone else edits the document or anyone has reserved it, and while a draft of yours that was ended waits to be taken back or discarded.
