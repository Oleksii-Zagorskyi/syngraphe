# History, comparing and undoing

## History

*History*, an icon beside the document's heading, lists a document's versions, newest first: each with its number, what happened — an edit, a reviewer's version, an acceptance, a return, an edition made canonical — who acted and when, and the freezes and unfreezes between them. A line marks where the accepted text ends: above it is the work not yet accepted.

What you see depends on your groups' rights. *View all history* shows every version your reading right reaches. *View previous canonical versions* shows the editions alone. *Edit* — and every right that includes it, *accept* among them — shows everything from the latest canonical edition up, the edition included — while the document has no edition, every version; nothing older opens, and the freezes are listed with *view all history* alone. Without any of these rights the icon is not shown. History lists, opens and compares; the undo stands on the review page.

## A version on its own page

A point of the document's path opens its version in the same window; *open*, from History, opens it in a new one, read only: a band across the top names it and links back to the current document. The path stands above it, the version's point outlined. The page has the outline, the appendices and the attachments as that version listed them — an appendix opens on a page of its own as that version had it — *Export* of that version from its icon, and *Compare with* the other versions you may see.

## Comparing two versions

A comparison shows the newer of two versions in full, with its changes against the older marked in place. Attachments cannot be compared: each is listed with its file in both versions and whether it was added, changed — its file, title or file name — removed or unchanged. On every comparison of two versions, *‹* — beside the older version's choice — opens the version before the older against it, and *›* — beside the newer's — the newer against the version after it, skipping the versions you may not see; each stands disabled where there is no such version. The outline of the newer text stands beside it, and the parts in the right pane.

*Show edit authors* names, in the right margin, who last edited each changed paragraph between the two versions, as on the review page; a paragraph moved but not changed names nobody. Across more than a hundred versions the names are not drawn, and the page says so. Two documents compared carry no names.

Appendices are compared in pairs. In the comparison's right pane, pick an appendix of each version and choose *Compare appendices*: the newer one is shown in full with its changes against the older marked, each named by its version and title, and *Back to the comparison* returns. The appendices of two different documents are compared the same way.

## Returning a run without accepting it

A reviewer who declines the edits waiting since the last acceptance chooses *Return without accepting…* on the review page and gives a reason: one line, required. The return is recorded in the document's own record, as an acceptance is. The edits stay pending; their authors are told, and read the reason in their notifications while they may still read the pending edits — the email names only the event. The document shows *returned* until a new version is sent, and then *awaiting review* again; *In review* leaves it out meanwhile. Nobody may return while the document is reserved.

## Undoing a version or an author's edits

A holder of *accept* can undo, from the panel of the review page, one version of the edits since the last acceptance — *Undo this edit* — or everything one author wrote since then — *Undo all of this author's edits since N.M*, on that author's newest version. The undo is typed into your own draft, which opens; nothing is removed from the history, and nothing becomes a version until you finish. Where a later version, or your draft, changed what the undone edits wrote, that place is left as it stands and listed above the editor, with the wording before the undone edits, what they wrote, and the wording now, for you to edit by hand. The undo covers each appendix as it covers the body, and a place left for you in an appendix is listed under its title. A title or a part changed by the undone edits is not undone here — an appendix they added or deleted among them; revert it from the review page.

Finishing with *Accept as* makes your version and accepts the edits; every author whose lines were removed receives a correction notice. *Create version* leaves it pending. While your undo waits in your draft, nobody accepts the edits from the review page: they are accepted with your *Accept as*. An undo waits while someone else edits the document or anyone has reserved it, and while a draft of yours that was ended waits to be taken back or discarded.
