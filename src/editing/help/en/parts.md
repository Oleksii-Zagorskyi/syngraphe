# Appendices and attachments

Beside its body, a document can have appendices and attachments. An **appendix** is a text of the document, written and read like the body and reviewed, accepted and frozen with it. An **attachment** is a file — a form, a table — kept with the document as it was uploaded and never opened by the platform.

## Reading them

**Parts**, in the right pane of a document's page, lists the appendices and the attachments of the version you are reading. An appendix opens on a page of its own: the document's title, then the appendix's, with the appendix's own outline and its text numbered from 1. The document's path stands above it, each point opening the appendix at that version, and its *Edit* opens the appendix's editor; the review page, *History* and *My edits* are the document's, which cover its appendices too. An attachment downloads as a file.

A version's page lists the appendices and attachments that version had, each opening or downloading as it was then.

## Changing them

While you hold the document, the **Parts** panel stands on the right of the editor: your draft's appendices and attachments, with the controls your rights allow.

- **Appendices.** *Add* creates one with its title; *rename* changes its title; *delete* drops it and its text from your draft; ↑ and ↓ move it up or down the list. An appendix is typed into from its own page's *Edit*, as the body is — the whole appendix at once. It starts with one top-level heading, and holds only that one.
- **Attachments.** *Add* uploads a file with its title; *replace* puts another file in its place; *rename* changes its title; *delete* drops it; ↑ and ↓ move it up or down the list. The download arrow beside it gives you the file as your draft holds it.

Adding needs *create appendix* or *upload attachment*, deleting *delete appendix* or *delete attachment*, replacing *replace attachment*; renaming and moving need only *edit*. A part you added yourself, never sent, can be deleted again with the right that added it; an appendix with an editor open is not deleted until the editor closes. The author of a document no group is linked to yet may upload and replace attachments.

Nothing changes in the document until you finish editing: the finishing screen and *My edits* list what your draft changes in the parts — each appendix's text marked as the body's is, and each part added, renamed, replaced, moved or deleted. If your rights changed since, finishing tells you which part it cannot keep.

## In review

The review page shows each appendix a version of the run changed after the body, its changes marked, and lists the parts each version changed. A reviewer may **revert** any of those part changes, one at a time — unless their own draft has already changed that part, which they settle first: the part goes back as it stood at the last acceptance, in the reviewer's own draft, and becomes part of their own version. While a revert waits in a draft, the edits are accepted only through that draft's *Accept as*.

When the edits are accepted, an author whose appendix or attachment a reviewer's version removed, restored, renamed or replaced is told in their correction notice, beside the lines.

## Exporting

An export can carry the appendices **embedded**, after the body — in PDF each starting a new page — or **separate**, each a file of its own in one zip. *Exporting* tells how.
