# Notifications

The bell at the top of every page opens **Notifications** and shows how many you have not read. Opening an entry marks it read and takes you to what it concerns.

- **Document.** What happened to the documents you work on: a version awaiting your review, while it still waits; your edits accepted; a **correction notice** when a reviewer changed lines you wrote; a document frozen, no longer frozen, or approved as an edition.
- **Administrative.** You were added to a group. Administrators are also told when a document is created — with *no reviewer assigned* beside it until a group that may accept is linked — and when an email could not be sent. A platform administrator is also told when the backup's state changes.

On the administrative tab, *Mark all as read* marks every entry read; an administrator also has filters there, each narrowing the list to one kind of event. Nobody is told of their own act.

**What an entry holds.** The document's number and title, the version's number, what happened — and who acted, where it was someone's version awaiting your review — and a link — never a line of the document's text or a change summary. An entry about a document you can no longer open is no longer listed or counted.

**By email.** Each notification — but one about an email that failed — is also sent as an email when your organisation or the platform has a sending address; the email holds what the entry holds, and its link opens the entry itself, marking it read. Without an address, notifications stay in the platform alone.

**The menu's views.** *In review* lists the documents awaiting review that you may accept, frozen ones included; a returned one leaves it until a version is sent after the return; *In edit* those you may edit whose text has moved beyond their latest edition. Each is shown only to someone who may accept, or edit, some document, and is drawn dimmed while it is empty.
