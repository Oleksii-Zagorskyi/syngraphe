# Editing together

## Held and reserved

While someone edits a document, it is *held*: nobody else can edit it, and everyone who may read it sees the tag *held* beside its title. Who is editing it is shown to those who may edit or accept it, and to administrators.

The person editing, if their group has the right *reserve for editing*, may reserve the document with *Reserve for editing*, beside *Finish editing* on their draft's point, to keep it for days or weeks. Everyone who may read it then sees *reserved for editing, since* the date it began; those who may edit or accept it, and the administrators, also see by whom. Until the reservation ends, review, freezing and changes to the document's groups wait. The person editing ends it by finishing, by discarding their edits, or by *Cancel the reservation*, which makes it ordinary editing again. Your reservations are listed under *My reservations* in the menu.

## When your editing is ended

When editing is left idle, *Are you still working?* is asked in an open editor and by a notification — a tab with no editor open is told by the notification alone. Answered, it carries on; unanswered, it ends ten minutes after the organisation's timeout. An administrator may also end someone's editing, from *More* on the document's page: ordinary editing by *Force release*; a reservation by *End the reservation*, with a reason the person editing reads in their notifications.

Your draft is kept if you had edited; editing ended with nothing edited keeps nothing. When you come back to the document, your kept draft is a point of its own on the path, which tells you who ended your editing, when and why. If the document has not changed since your draft began — no new version and no acceptance — *Take the document back* lets you carry on with your draft. Otherwise your draft can only be read, as changes against the text you choose; carry over what you need by hand, then *Discard this draft…*.

## Editing in two tabs

Editing opens the whole document's editor at once. If you edit the document in two tabs, an editor whose text was changed in the other stops: it keeps what you typed and shows it as changes against the text as it now stands, for you to carry over. *Finish editing* waits until the other tabs editing the document are closed.

## Discarding

*Discard my edits…*, on your draft's point or on the finishing screen, throws the whole draft away — its text and its files — after you confirm in its window. Before your first edit the act is *Release the document* instead: it lets the document go, and there is nothing to keep. A reservation ends with either.

## Unfreezing

A holder of the right *freeze and unfreeze* ends a freeze with *Unfreeze*, on the latest accepted version of the path. Editing resumes at the same accepted number.
