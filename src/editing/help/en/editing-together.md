# Editing together

## Held and reserved

While someone edits a document, it is *held*: nobody else can edit it, and everyone who may read it sees the tag *held* beside its title. Who is editing it is shown to administrators alone: the organisation's, and a platform administrator acting in it.

The person editing, if their group has the right *reserve for editing*, may reserve the document from the edit tab, to keep it for days or weeks. Everyone who may read it then sees *reserved for editing, since* the date it began; those who may edit or accept it, and the administrators, also see by whom. Until the reservation ends, review, freezing and changes to the document's groups wait. The person editing ends it by finishing, by discarding their edits, or by *Cancel the reservation*, which makes it ordinary editing again. Your reservations are listed under *My reservations* in the menu.

## When your editing is ended

Editing left without activity is asked *Are you still working?* in an open editor, and by a notification — a tab with no editor open is told by the notification alone. Answered, it carries on; unanswered, it ends ten minutes after the organisation's timeout. An administrator may also end someone's editing: ordinary editing by *Force release*; a reservation by *End the reservation*, with a reason the person editing reads in their notifications.

Your draft is kept. When you come back to the edit tab, it tells you who ended your editing, when and why. If the document has not changed since your draft began — no new version and no acceptance — *Take the document back* lets you carry on with your draft. Otherwise your draft can only be read, as changes against the text you choose; carry over what you need by hand, then *Discard this draft*.

## Editing in two tabs

Editing opens the whole document's editor at once. If you edit the document in two tabs, an editor whose text was changed in the other stops: it keeps what you typed and shows it as changes against the text as it now stands, for you to carry over. *Finish editing* waits until the other tabs editing the document are closed.

## Discarding

*Discard my edits*, in the edit tab or on the finishing screen, throws the whole draft away — its text and its files — after you confirm. A reservation ends with it.

## Unfreezing

A holder of the right *freeze and unfreeze* ends a freeze with *Unfreeze*. Editing resumes at the same accepted number.
