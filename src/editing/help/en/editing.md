# Editing

The editor has two views of the same text: the formatted view and the source view, which shows the text as Markdown with each paragraph on one line. Switch between them with the control above the text.

There is no Save button. What you type is kept a moment after you pause, and the indicator above the text says whether it was. If you close the page, your text is there when you come back.

Text pasted from elsewhere is checked. A construct the editor does not accept is refused with its line named; in the source view a conversion is offered, shown before it is applied. The list of what the editor accepts is on its own page.

When you are done, choose *Finish editing*.

## Images

Choose *Image* in the toolbar to add a picture from your computer, or paste or drop one into the text. PNG, JPEG and GIF are accepted, up to 10 MB, or less where the platform's upload maximum is lower, and at most 40 megapixels and 10,000 pixels a side; a picture refused says why. Each image stands as a paragraph of its own, with a short description that a reader who cannot see it reads instead. Before an image is kept, everything in its file but the picture is taken out — the camera, the place, any comment — except, in a JPEG, which way up it stands. An image that is only a web address is not pasted: copy the image itself and paste it.

## Roles, actions and terms

Select words and choose *Mark* to mark them as a role, an action or a term of this document: pick one the document already declares, or declare a new one by its label. Words already marked take the new one. A role, action or term typed in the source view with a key the document does not declare is declared with your version, labelled with its words. When you finish, and in *My edits*, the roles, actions and terms your draft adds, relabels or removes are listed.

## Mentions

Select words and choose *Mention*, then type another document's number, to mention that document. A reader who may read it sees your words as a link to it; any other reader sees your words alone. Nothing about the document is looked up, so check the number.
