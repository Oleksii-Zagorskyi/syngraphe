# Reviewing and accepting

Every version sent since the last acceptance waits in **pending edits**; together they are the *run* a reviewer reviews. A reviewer — a holder of *accept* — reads it and decides.

- **The review page.** *Review the run*, on the latest version of the document's path, opens it, as does the arc of the edits waiting for review. It shows the whole document with every change since the last acceptance marked in place, its outline beside it, and on the right a panel of the versions under review — a list, each with its number, which opens that version's page, its change summary whole, author and date. Tick *Show edit authors*, at the head of the right margin, to see beside each changed paragraph who last edited it — here, and on a past version's page against the version before it; a paragraph with no change names nobody, and one whose sentences have several authors names each at its sentence's end. A change of formatting alone is marked *formatting only* and folded away; open it to see the earlier form. Everyone else who may read the pending edits opens the same page from the arc, without its acts.
- **Edit** takes the document so you can change what you will not accept as it stands, as any editing does. Your changes become a version of your own. Finish with **accept as N.M** — the version is made and everything pending accepted in one act — or with **create version N.M-X**, which leaves it waiting with the others. In the panel, *Undo this edit* on a version and *Undo all of this author's edits since N.M* on an author's newest put the undo into your draft: *History, comparing and undoing* tells how.
- **Parts.** The review page shows each appendix a version changed after the body, its changes marked, and lists the parts each version added, renamed, replaced, moved or deleted. **Revert** takes the document and puts one of those part changes back as it stood at the last acceptance, as part of your own version — unless your own draft has already changed that part. While a revert waits in a draft, the edits are accepted only through that draft's *Accept as*.
- **Accept N — becomes M** accepts every version since the last acceptance in one act; the confirmation names them and the number that results. **Return without accepting…** sends them back with a reason. Either is refused while someone has the document reserved for editing.
- **What the author is told.** When pending edits are accepted, every author whose lines a reviewer's version changed — or whose removal it undid — receives one correction notice: each line as they wrote it and as it was accepted, and who changed it — and each appendix or attachment of theirs a reviewer's version removed, restored, renamed or replaced. The notice's line among the author's notifications says how many lines and parts changed; the notice itself shows them. Nobody is told about their own lines, and a moved line is not a changed one. The notice keeps no text: the wording is read from the document each time it is opened, and shows only while you may read the document's accepted text.
