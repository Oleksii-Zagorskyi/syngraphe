# Decision Log — Syngraphē

Append-only log of the product's design decisions. New rows go at the top of the table. A decision is changed by a new row that supersedes it, never by editing the old one. Rows decided on a branch built beside another carry that branch's epic number — `DL-10-001` — and keep it after the merge; the rest follow one sequence.

The rows of release 0.1 are in [`decision-log-0.1.md`](decision-log-0.1.md); the ids continue from it.
